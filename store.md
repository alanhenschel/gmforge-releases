# GMForge AI Co-Pilot for Owlbear Rodeo 2.0

**GMForge** unifies the three highest-impact Game Master workflows directly inside your Owlbear Rodeo 2.0 room:

1. **Instant Encounter & Statblock Builder (Pre-Session & Live):**
   - Paste any adventure room excerpt or prompt (*"Burning tavern ambush with 1 Mercenary Captain CR 4 and 3 Goblin Archers"*) to generate structured **D&D 5e (2014/2024)** and **Pathfinder 2e Remaster** statblocks with clickable dice actions, inline HP tracking, and combat tactics.
   - Spawn tokens and statblocks directly into your active Owlbear Rodeo scene in 1 click.

2. **Live GM Co-Pilot & Interactive Shops (During & Between Sessions):**
   - Generate rich improv NPCs in under 2 seconds (voice/mannerisms, motivation, and GM-only secrets hidden from screen share by default).
   - Open an **Interactive Shop** or Loot Pile for your players with automatic `GP / SP / CP` coin deduction and an off-session mobile **Player Hub** for downtime shopping.

3. **AI Session Scribe & Auto-Wiki Sync (Post-Session):**
   - Record multi-track Discord voice sessions with built-in player consent & opt-out controls (`/record start`, `/record optout`) and automatically extract session recaps, timelines, NPCs, locations, quests, and loot.

## How to Use
- Click the **GMForge Co-Pilot** icon in your Owlbear Rodeo top bar to open the `460×720` popover drawer.
- Switch between **Scribe**, **Encounter**, and **Shops/NPC** tabs at any time during prep or live play.
